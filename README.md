# Federal Contract Indirect Rate Reconciliation

Calculate final indirect rates, reconcile provisional billings, and recover allowable contract revenue.

**Primary buyer:** Federal contractors and professional services firms. **Evidence:** contracts, provisional billing rates, direct labor, indirect pools, allocation bases, unallowable costs, invoices, final rates, and payments.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Contract clause library
- Fiscal year rate structure
- Direct cost ingestion
- Fringe pool calculation
- Overhead pool calculation
- G&A pool calculation
- Allocation base validation
- Unallowable cost exclusion
- Provisional billing reconciliation
- Final rate calculation
- Funding ceiling control
- Completion invoice preparation
- Government negotiation support
- Cash recovery tracking
- Contract rate analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4693>. API: `5693`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
